# Code of conduct

SpriteKiln follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment,
insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/LucasJRamos). Reports are handled confidentially, and the
maintainer may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
